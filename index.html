About the Works

A Faridabad workshop, running since 2015.

Founded in 2015 in Faridabad, Haryana, New Tech Engg. Works is a sole-proprietorship manufacturing unit. We began with fibreglass work for e-rickshaws, roof ventilators and air-cooler bodies, and have since built out a full line of FRP planters and gamlas for homes, hotels and landscaping projects — all produced in the same works, to order.

Every quote is worked out against the piece, the finish and the volume — there is no fixed catalogue price, because no two orders leave the workshop the same way.
